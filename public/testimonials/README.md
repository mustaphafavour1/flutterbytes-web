# Testimonials

Drop testimonial screenshots here — social-media posts where people share their
FlutterBytes experience. They appear (by default) in the gallery tabs on the
homepage and on the `/gallery` page; visitors can switch to the "Photos" tab to
see event pictures.

## Naming

- Any name works: `tweet-ada.png`, `linkedin-1.jpg`, etc.
- Any image extension: `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`, …
- **Optional `N-` prefix** to control order (lower = first): `1-featured.png`.

Screenshots can be any size/aspect — they lay out in a masonry grid. Portrait or
square social screenshots look best.
